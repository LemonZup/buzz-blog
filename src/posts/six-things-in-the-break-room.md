Roll a six-sided die when the expedition finds somewhere that looks comfortable. Give the room a little time to feel welcoming before revealing its problem.

## 1. The kettle

Already boiling. Unplugging it does nothing. Anyone who makes tea hears a conversation that will happen here tomorrow.

## 2. The noticeboard

A photograph of the current party, celebrating someone's retirement. The date is next week. One face has been neatly cut out.

## 3. The vending machine

It takes apologies. A sincere one buys a warm meal. An insincere one buys a small, sealed envelope addressed to the person you wronged.

## 4. The spare coat

Exactly your size. Its pockets contain the things you will lose during this expedition. Taking an item out makes it disappear from wherever you currently keep it.

## 5. The window

Shows the outside of this building from across the street. Someone is standing in your place, looking back. They leave a moment before you do.

## 6. The birthday cake

Freshly cut, with enough slices for everyone. The candles relight whenever someone lies. There is no obvious way for the cake to hear you.

## Using the room

Pick one detail and let it matter. The kettle might reveal a useful warning. The photograph might become a reason to return. A strange room earns its place when the group can make a decision about it.
